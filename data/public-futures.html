Nifty Future

The Nifty future doesn't move in isolation—it opens each morning already carrying the weight of what happened overnight. Gift Nifty futures are the first to react, trading through the early hours and effectively previewing where Nifty futures are likely to open, often within a few points of the actual price. But Gift Nifty itself takes its cue from global markets, and the biggest of those is the US. Dow Jones index futures trade nearly 24 hours, so a sharp overnight move on the Dow can lift or drag Gift Nifty, which then flows straight into the Nifty future's opening price and early momentum. That's why seasoned traders check all three in sequence—Dow futures first, then Gift Nifty, then the Nifty future live chart—to see whether the opening gap has real global backing or is likely to fade. To follow that chain from the top, visit Dow Jones index futures and get an early read on global sentiment before the Nifty session begins.
